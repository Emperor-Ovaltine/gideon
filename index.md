## About

Gideon transforms your Discord server into an AI-powered hub, connecting members to state-of-the-art language and image models. Have intelligent conversations, generate images, play trivia, manage reminders, and more.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Provider AI Chat** - OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and 100+ models via OpenRouter
- **Image Generation** - AI Horde, Cloudflare Workers, DALL-E, ComfyUI, and OpenRouter
- **AI-Powered Trivia** - Dynamic questions on any topic with scoring and leaderboards
- **Thread Management** - Dedicated AI conversation threads with per-thread settings
- **Natural Language Intents** - @mention the bot to set reminders, generate images, or search the web
- **Web Admin Dashboard** - Browser-based management interface
- **URL Summarization** - Instant summaries of shared links
